        How to play

        Hold the screen, mouse or Space to crouch and charge (up to 1 second), then release to leap. A quick tap is a small hop; a full charge launches you high with more time for tricks.
        Swipe in the air to do tricks. On desktop just flick the mouse or trackpad, no clicking. Two-part swipes (✓, L, up-down) make harder tricks. Hold Shift, or keep a second finger on the screen, for the tucked versions.
        Tap again just before the water to lock in a straight entry. Less splash = more points from the three judges (each out of 10).

          Win a round to unlock the next, higher-stakes competition. Three rivals dive after you.

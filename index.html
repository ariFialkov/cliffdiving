        How to play

        Hold the screen, mouse or Space to crouch and charge (up to 1 second), then release to leap. A quick tap is a small hop; a full charge launches you high with more time for tricks.
        Swipe in the air to do tricks. On desktop just flick the mouse or trackpad, no clicking. Two-part swipes (✓, L, up-down) make harder tricks. Hold Shift while flicking, or keep a second finger on the screen, for the tucked versions. Fire a second trick right away for a combo.
        Hold the screen, mouse button or Shift in the air to rotate forward in a straight dive. Let go when you're lined up: feet-first, or head-first for less splash. Hard swipes over-rotate, soft ones under-rotate, so fix it up before you hit the water.
        Tap just before the water to streamline. Judges (each out of 10) score difficulty, how straight you went in and your streamline timing.

          Win a round to unlock the next, higher-stakes competition. Three rivals dive after you.
